Submission: Sensor: DHT22 Temperature and Humidity Sensor
What it does

The DHT22 sensor measures temperature and humidity in the environment. It is commonly used in IoT projects like smart homes, weather stations, and greenhouse monitoring.

Electronics / Hardware inside
Capacitive humidity sensor (detects moisture levels in air)
Thermistor (measures temperature)
Microcontroller chip (converts analog signals into digital data)
Analog or Digital
Outputs digital signal (no need for analog-to-digital conversion)
Units and Range
Temperature: °C, range -40°C to 80°C
Humidity: % (Relative Humidity), range 0–100% RH
Accuracy: about ±0.5°C (temperature), ±2–5% RH (humidity)

What it does

A servo motor is used to control precise movement or position, such as rotating an arm, opening a door, or steering a robot.

Electronics / Hardware inside
DC motor
Gear system (to increase torque and control movement)
Control circuit
Potentiometer (to measure position/angle)
Analog or Digital
Controlled by digital PWM (Pulse Width Modulation) signal
Units and Range
Position measured in degrees (°)
Typical range: 0° to 180°
Controlled by pulse signals (e.g., 1ms–2ms pulse width)
Conclusion

The DHT22 sensor is useful for collecting environmental data (input), while the servo motor acts on commands to create movement (output). Together, they show how IoT systems can both sense and interact with the physical world..
